How to opt in

The Sean Stewart Personal Assistant is a private service with a single user: its owner, Sean Stewart. The number is not advertised.

Opt-in by keyword

To start receiving messages, the user texts the keyword START to (415) 650-4786, or simply texts or calls the number with a question. The assistant only ever replies to the owner's verified mobile number.

After texting START, the user receives this confirmation:

Sean Stewart Personal Assistant: You're opted in to replies from your personal assistant. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages. Reply HELP for help.
