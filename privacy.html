Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy covers the Sean Stewart Personal Assistant text-message service, operated by Sean Stewart (Sole Proprietor). Last updated October 17, 2026.

What we collect

The service receives the text messages and voice messages sent to (888) 635-4106 by its single user, the owner, along with the sending phone number and timestamps. Voice messages are transcribed to text and the recording is then deleted.

How it is used

This information is used only to answer the user's questions and carry out tasks the user requests, such as looking up information or saving a note, and to reply by text message.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Messages are processed by the service providers needed to run the assistant (the SMS carrier and messaging platform, and the AI and transcription providers) solely to provide the service.

Opting out

Reply STOP to (888) 635-4106 at any time to stop messages. Reply HELP for help.
